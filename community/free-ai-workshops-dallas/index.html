The series

From watching an AI Agent to designing a real workflow

What is the Dallas community AI workshop series? It is a free sequence of practical, Chinese-language events organized by Jack Qian for professionals, founders, educators, creators, students, and other people who want to apply AI to real work. The series began in Plano on April 2, continued in Dallas on May 17, and has a third Dallas session scheduled for July 18, 2026.

Each event advances the learning path. The first showed an AI Agent in action. The second explained how several agents can divide professional work. The third focuses on building and demonstrating repeatable AI automation workflows.

Choose an event format

AI class, workshop, meetup, or bootcamp: which one fits?

Dallas has several ways to learn AI. The best choice depends on whether you want a credential, a career change, a guided practice session, or a local community.

AI class or course

Choose a structured course when you want a fixed curriculum, regular assignments, or a certificate. Check the prerequisites, total hours, and full cost before enrolling.

Hands-on AI workshop

Choose a workshop when you want to practice one useful topic in a few hours. AI Man Jack's sessions focus on live tools, real workflows, questions, and demonstrations.

AI meetup or event

Choose a meetup when peer learning, local connections, and project demos matter most. This series combines guided teaching with time for community discussion.

A technical bootcamp is usually longer and designed for career preparation. AI Man Jack's Dallas events are currently free community workshops and meetups, not a certification bootcamp.
