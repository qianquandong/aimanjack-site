My approach to AI education is the opposite of hype. I take one real way a working person is using an AI agent today, and I break it all the way down: the exact tool, the exact prompt, what it saves in time, and where it breaks so you don't get burned. If you can copy, paste, and send a text message, you can use what I teach.

What you'll actually learn

  • AI agents for everyday work: drafting email, summarizing long documents, cleaning up spreadsheets, researching a prospect before a call.
  • Which tool for which job: when a chatbot is enough, when you need an agent that takes actions, and when AI is the wrong tool entirely.
  • Prompts that hold up: how to ask so the answer is useful the first time, and how to catch the confident-but-wrong answers.
  • Guardrails: what not to paste into an AI tool, and how to keep your customers' information safe.
