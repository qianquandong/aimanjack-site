Common questions

Can it book with a specific stylist?

Yes on Growth and Pro, inside the hours and services you set for that stylist. Starter answers questions and takes messages but does not book.

Will it quote prices?

Only the starting prices you approved. For anything that depends on hair length or condition, it says so and offers a consultation slot.

Does it take deposits?

No payments are taken on the call. It states your deposit policy and you collect the deposit the way you do today.

What about walk-ins?

It states your walk-in policy and offers the next open slot if you allow it.
