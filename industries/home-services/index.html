Common questions

Can it quote a job?

Only flat items you approved, such as a trip fee or a diagnostic. Everything else becomes a captured estimate request with the details you asked for.

Does it book arrival windows?

Yes on Growth and Pro, using the windows and capacity you define.

What about after-hours emergencies?

Your rule decides: text you the details immediately, transfer to an on-call number, or take a message for the morning.

Does it assign technicians?

No. It captures the job and books the window; you dispatch.
