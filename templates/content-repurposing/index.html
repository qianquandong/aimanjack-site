Content Repurposing Workflow Template

A two-step prompt: extract the five strongest claims from a finished piece, then write one LinkedIn post, one email and one short script — one claim each.

TeamMarketing
ForContent marketer · Founder
ToolsChatGPT · Claude
The template · copy and paste
STEP 1 — run this first
Read SOURCE. List the 5 most specific claims in it.
After each claim, quote the sentence that supports it.
Stop and wait for me to choose three.

STEP 2 — after I choose
For each chosen claim write ONE format:

LINKEDIN POST
- Under 120 words. Line 1 is the claim. Short paragraphs.
- No hashtags, no emojis. End with a genuine question.

EMAIL
- Subject line under 45 characters
- Under 100 words, one link, one ask

60-SECOND SCRIPT
- Spoken language. Sentences under 15 words.
- Hook in the first 5 seconds, one example, one takeaway

RULES
- Facts only from SOURCE. No new statistics.
- Match the voice of MY SAMPLES.
- Delete any opening sentence that is a warm-up.

MY SAMPLES
[PASTE TWO PIECES YOU LIKE]

SOURCE
[PASTE THE FINISHED PIECE]
Before you start

What you need first

  • One finished long piece you own
  • Two short pieces in your voice
Four steps

How to use it

  1. 1

    Run step 1 and choose claims you would defend out loud.

  2. 2

    Assign one claim per format. Do not let one post carry all three.

  3. 3

    Cut the first sentence of each draft before publishing.

Customise

Make it yours

  • Swap formats for your channels (newsletter intro, sales one-liner, slide).
  • Add "banned words: …" with the phrases your brand never uses.

This template is the reusable half of the Content Repurposing with AI workflow, which covers the full process, an example output and the review checklist. More for this team: AI for Marketing. Want it adapted to your team’s work? See how training works.
