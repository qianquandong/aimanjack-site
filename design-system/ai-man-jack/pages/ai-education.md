# AI education page override

## Purpose

Present AI Education as a no-cost Dallas community initiative focused on public learning, documented events, and practical resources.

## Required hierarchy

1. Free community education promise.
2. Evidence from completed and scheduled events.
3. What participants learn and who the program serves.
4. Ways to attend, invite a session, or use free resources.
5. Clear disclosure that the initiative is currently offered at no charge.

## Avoid

- Pricing language.
- Sales-first calls to action.
- Implying legal nonprofit status.
- Describing scheduled events as completed.
