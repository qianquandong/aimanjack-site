# Home page override

## Purpose

The home page is a two-audience gateway. It must explain the shared mission, then separate the no-cost community education initiative from paid business implementation.

## Required hierarchy

1. Umbrella value proposition: practical AI for Dallas communities and small businesses.
2. Two equal, clearly labeled paths: Free AI Education and Paid Business Services.
3. First-party community proof: events, hackathon, photographs, and transparent evidence notes.
4. Business proof: live websites, concrete deliverables, transparent pricing.
5. Founder story connecting both paths.

## Interaction rules

- Entire path cards are clickable and have visible hover/focus states.
- Do not mix education CTAs with pricing CTAs.
- Use plain language; avoid presenting free education as a commercial training package.
- On small screens, keep the two primary navigation paths visible and hide only optional links already available in page content.
