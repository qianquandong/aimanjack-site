Common questions

The free checkup, answered plainly

Why free? What's the catch?

The checkup is how I earn the right to a conversation. If it finds problems and you want them fixed, that work is paid — I build you a done-for-you AI customer service and booking system, website included, for $2,000 one-time, then $200 a month to keep it answering customers and booking appointments 24/7. If you fix them yourself or do nothing, the page is still yours. No follow-up spam.

Do you need my passwords or my Google account?

No. Everything in the checkup comes from what customers can already see: your Google listing, your website, your reviews. Nothing needs your password.

What do I actually get?

One printed-style page: where you stand on Google Maps next to the three shops beside you, whether AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend you or a competitor, and the three things costing you customers — each with a real number, not opinions.

Why does it matter what ChatGPT says about my shop?

More and more people now ask AI assistants like ChatGPT to find local businesses — and most shops never come up in those answers. Your Google ranking no longer tells the whole story — the checkup shows both.

Why does ChatGPT recommend my competitor instead of me?

Usually not because they're better. An assistant answers from text it can find and confirm, so it names the business it has the most agreeing information about: a Google Business Profile with the right category and real services listed, hours and phone that match on every site, recent reviews with replies, and a website that says in plain words what the shop does and where. A shop with three of those beats a better shop with none of them. The checkup shows which ones you're missing.

How do I get my business to show up in AI search?

Write down the facts an assistant needs and put them where it can read them. That means a claimed and complete Google Business Profile, one consistent name, address, and phone everywhere, service pages that name the actual service and the actual city instead of "quality service you can trust," and answers to the questions customers really ask. There is no ad to buy and no way to pay for the spot — it's whichever business is easiest to verify.

Which businesses is this for?

Local shops around Dallas, Richardson, Plano, Garland, Allen, and Melissa: hair and nail salons, barbershops, auto repair, pet groomers, restaurants, and home service trades.
