Common questions

Missed call text back, answered plainly

What is missed call text back?

When someone calls your business and nobody picks up, the system automatically sends that caller a text message within about ten seconds. The text says you missed them and gives them a link to book. Instead of calling the next shop on Google, they book with you.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep the number that is on your door, your Google listing, and every card you have handed out. Setup is one carrier code dialed on your own phone that forwards unanswered calls into the service. It takes about two minutes at your counter, and there is no app to install.

How much does it cost in Dallas?

Forty-nine dollars a month, no setup fee, cancel anytime. Local agencies typically bundle this into packages costing a few hundred dollars a month. One recovered customer usually covers the whole month.

Does it work with Square or my existing booking system?

Yes. The text sends whatever booking link you already use, including Square. Nothing about your current booking, point-of-sale, or calendar setup changes.

What happens when the customer texts back?

Their reply lands on your phone as a normal text, and whatever you send back goes to them. It reads like an ordinary conversation on both ends.

Which businesses does this help most?

Appointment businesses where the owner is working with their hands and cannot grab the phone: hair and nail salons, barbershops, auto repair shops, pet groomers, cleaners, and home service trades around Dallas, Richardson, Plano, Garland, and Allen.
