How engagement works

From first call to a workflow that survives

  1. Step 1

    A 30-minute call

    What your team does every week, which tools are approved, what you want people to be able to do.

  2. Step 2

    A recommended format and a fixed price. No add-ons.

  3. Step 3

    The session, on your work

    Each person builds one workflow on a task they already do, and catches one deliberately flawed output.

  4. Step 4

    A two-week check

    Was it tested, was it accurate enough to use, should it continue, change or stop.
