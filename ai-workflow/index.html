What is AI workflow automation?

AI workflow automation connects a clear trigger to a repeatable action and a result a person can review. For a Dallas service business, that might mean detecting a missed call, sending a useful text with the booking link, recording the response, and alerting the owner when human follow-up is needed.

You pay your software to manage the customers you have. I help follow up with the ones you do not.

Square, Vagaro, or whatever you run is great at handling the customers already in front of you: booking them, charging them, keeping their record. What it doesn't do is chase the ones who almost became customers and slipped away. That's exactly what an AI workflow is for. It's not a replacement for your system; it bolts on top and does the follow-up work you never have time for.
