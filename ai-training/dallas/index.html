Common questions

AI training in Dallas–Fort Worth, answered plainly

Do you come onsite to Plano, Frisco or Fort Worth?

Yes. Onsite training runs at your office anywhere in Dallas–Fort Worth — Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Richardson, Frisco, McKinney, Irving, Arlington and the cities between. Elsewhere in Texas is available on request, with travel quoted separately. Remote sessions over video are available anywhere in the U.S.

How many people can you train at once?

Group size and practice arrangements are agreed in the written team quote. Share the number attending, their roles and experience levels. Larger groups may need department blocks or separate sessions so there is enough time for practice and feedback.

What does AI training cost in Dallas?

Every team gets its own quote: it depends on group size, the format and how much preparation your workflows need. The four-session program, the 90-minute team workshop and the full onsite day are all priced in writing before anything is agreed. Use the quote form or book a 20-minute call.

Is the training in English or Chinese?

Either. The 2026 community series in Dallas ran in Chinese; company sessions run in whichever language the room works in, and the materials come in both.

Which AI tools do you teach?

Whichever your company has already approved — ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude or Gemini. If none is approved yet, we agree on one before the first session. The method is the same across tools, and the first thing covered is which data may go into a tool and which may not.

What do we need to prepare?

Each attendee identifies two or three recurring tasks and brings a laptop with access to the approved AI tool. Exercises use synthetic, redacted or company-approved sample material. Onsite training also needs a room with a screen and reliable Wi-Fi. No coding experience is required; the four-session program includes practice between meetings.

How is this different from a public AI class in Dallas?

The session is built around your team’s tasks and company data rules. Participants practise spotting errors before using an output. The four-session program includes a review two weeks after the final session; additional support after a standalone workshop or onsite day is separately scoped.

Can we attend a community session before booking?

Yes. The community sessions in Dallas are free and listed on Luma, where you can see the next date. Come, watch how a session runs, then decide. 309 people signed up for them in 2026.
