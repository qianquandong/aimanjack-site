Training method

Practise the task—and how to check it

Exercises follow a practical sequence.

Exercises use synthetic, redacted or company-approved sample material. Tool selection and data rules are agreed before the session.

  1. 1

    Choose a suitable task.

    Identify recurring work with clear inputs and an output someone can check.

  2. 2

    Provide useful context.

    Supply approved material, explain the constraints and specify the required format.

  3. 3

    Build repeatable steps.

    Write instructions the team can test and revise.

  4. 4

    Review the output.

    Check facts, missing context and unsupported claims, including practice with a deliberately flawed example.

  5. 5

    Set clear boundaries.

    Agree which information can be used and where a person must approve the result.

Questions & preparation

Before we start

Which tools will we use?

Training uses tools your company has approved, which may include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot or Grok. The selection depends on your team’s tasks and access.

What should participants prepare?

Bring a few recurring tasks and suitable sample material. Participants need a laptop and access to the agreed tool. Onsite sessions also require reliable Wi-Fi and a shared screen or projector. Onsite preparation details.

Does training include software implementation?

Training does not include software development or CRM/ERP configuration and integration. Connecting to live systems or automatically updating records requires a separate implementation scope, permissions and testing.

Discuss a starting point for your team

Bring one recurring task to a workflow call. Discuss what your team needs, which tools are available and which training format fits the work.
