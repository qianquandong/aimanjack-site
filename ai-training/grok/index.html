Agents need tighter rules than chat

A chat answer can be ignored; an agent that signs in to your tools can send, file and change things. So we start with the smallest scope that does the job, a person approving every outgoing action until the agent has earned trust, and separate accounts where possible. For xAI’s business security and data controls, see xAI’s business page ↗; we check the current terms of the plan you choose before the session.

Questions

Grok training, answered

What is Grok Bot?

Grok Bot is xAI’s product for AI agents: you give a Bot a job, it signs in to your tools, uses them the way you do, and comes back with finished work or drafts for you to approve. It is newer than chat tools, so the training focuses on scoping a job, approvals and what an agent may access.

Is Grok suitable for company use?

For some work, on a business plan. Its strength is real-time search across the web and X. We cover the business account options and check the current data terms with you before anyone puts company material in.

Is it safe to let an AI agent use our accounts?

Only with limits. We give each agent one job and the minimum access it needs, hold anything that sends, pays or deletes for a person’s approval, and review its actions for the first weeks. Some tasks should never be handed to an agent, and we say which.

Do you teach Grok on its own or alongside other tools?

Usually alongside. Many teams use Grok for live research and another tool for documents and writing; see ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot training. The method is the same across tools.

How do we know the sources Grok cites are right?

We practise it: open the cited source, find the exact sentence, check the date. Posts on X are opinions or news, not facts, until a second source confirms them.

What does an agent rollout look like after the training?

Start with one low-risk job, approvals on every action, and a weekly review of what the agent did. Widen its scope only when its work stops needing corrections.

What does each person leave with?

At least one Grok research workflow or one scoped Bot job on a real task, written down with its approval steps, and a short checklist of what to verify.
