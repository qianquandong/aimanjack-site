AI for Marketing

Research, create, repurpose and analyse content faster — without sounding like everyone else. Each workflow has steps, a prompt and a review checklist.

The short answer

Marketing teams use AI best where there is source material to work from: interview transcripts to synthesise, competitor pages to compare, a finished piece to repurpose. Starting from a blank prompt produces the generic copy every other team is also publishing.
